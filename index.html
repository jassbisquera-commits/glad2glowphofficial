Before you apply

Shade & skin type guide

Our base makeup range spans a wide shade library formulated for a range of undertones and skin types. Most creators find their match using their current foundation shade as a reference point.

Not sure which shade to request? Mention your usual foundation shade, undertone, and skin type in your application notes, and our team will confirm your match before anything ships.

As with any new cosmetic product, we recommend a patch test 24–48 hours before full use, especially if you have sensitive skin.

Before you apply

Who we're looking for

What we look for

  • Based in the Philippines, 18 years or older
  • Active on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts with consistent posting
  • A content style that fits soft, natural, everyday beauty
  • Comfortable filming GRWM, skincare routines, or makeup tutorials
  • Genuine engagement — comments and saves matter more to us than raw follower count

What isn't required

  • No minimum follower count to apply
  • No professional makeup artistry experience needed
  • No cost to apply or to join the program, ever
  • No obligation to feature every product offered to you

Before you apply

Creator program details

A simple, transparent overview of what selected creators can expect from the glad2glow program.

Product access

Selected creators may receive campaign products based on the active brief, creator tier, and campaign availability.

Content freedom

Create in your own style. Campaign briefs provide the essentials while keeping your content natural and authentic.

Grow with the program

Consistent creators may be considered for recurring campaigns, higher tiers, and paid collaboration opportunities.

Application review

Applications are reviewed based on content quality, consistency, audience fit, and the needs of each campaign.

Good to know: There is no follower minimum to apply. A clear profile, consistent content, and genuine interest in beauty matter more than follower count.
